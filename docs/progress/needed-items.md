# Needed items
{: .no_toc }

Everything your remaining quests still ask you to hand over or plant, compared with what you have.

1. TOC
{:toc}

---

## The table

Each row sums one item across all remaining quests, next to how many you hold in your stash and how many
of those are found in raid. Expand a row to see which quests need it.

<!-- screenshot: needed items table with one row expanded -->

## Filters

- **Category**: the icon row above the table filters by item category (ammo, keys, electronics, ...)
- **Missing**: items you still lack
- **In stash (don't sell)**: items you already have and should keep
- **FIR required**: items that must be found in raid
- **Search**: find an item by name

{: .note }
When a quest accepts any one of several items, they count as a single line. Quests you can't reach
(other faction, a branch you've closed) are left out of the totals.

## What counts as "in stash"

Your stash and the gear you're wearing, the same as the in-game hand-over window. Hideout stashes are not counted.
