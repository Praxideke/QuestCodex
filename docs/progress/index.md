# Progress

The Progress menu shows where a profile stands and what it still needs. It has three pages:

| Page | What it does |
|:-----|:-------------|
| [Overview](overview.html) | Trader progress, quests you can act on now, quests by status |
| [Raid prep](raid-prep.html) | Everything to do on the map you're about to play |
| [Needed items](needed-items.html) | Items your remaining quests still ask for, against what you have |

## Choosing a profile

The profile bar at the top is shared by all three pages. It starts on the profile that is currently online
and remembers your choice in the browser.

<!-- screenshot: profile bar -->

## Live updates

The pages refresh on their own when the game saves your profile (for example after a raid or a turn-in)
and when you come back to the tab. Quests that changed are briefly highlighted.
